# Business Model and Product Positioning

## Product statement

> **Aegis Atlas gives authorized assessment teams one map-first workspace to coordinate scope, technical evidence, exposure relationships, facility context, and decision-ready reporting.**

The differentiated value is not a generic map or a generic finding tracker. It is the ability to explain a security decision through geography, dependency, evidence provenance, scope, ownership, and remediation status in the same operational surface.

## Initial customers

| Buyer | Operational problem | Aegis Atlas outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Security consultancies | Repeating assessment reporting and client coordination across engagements | Reusable engagement templates, governed evidence, GIS reporting, and auditable delivery workflow |
| Internal offensive-security teams | Scope drift, fragmented evidence, and unclear asset/provider context | A controlled workspace that joins scope, findings, geospatial context, tasks, and retest workflow |
| Infrastructure and resilience teams | Limited visibility into geographic/provider concentration | Dependency, site, region, uncertainty, and concentration context for decision discussions |
| Enterprise security leadership | Findings reports are difficult to interpret and prioritize | Executive report views that preserve the connection between evidence, impact, owner, and geography |

## Packaging hypothesis

| Package | Value unit | Included capability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Assessment Workspace | Per engagement | Scope, assets, imports, evidence metadata, map, reports, and retest workflow |
| Operations Portfolio | Per team | Engagement health, analyst tasking, queue management, governed report drafts, and portfolio analytics |
| Enterprise Governance | Annual platform | Private deployment, organization identity integration, fine-grained roles, retention, audit export, integrations, and support commitments |

## Business metrics to measure

The early product should measure time from authorization to evidence-ready report, percentage of findings with complete provenance, remediation task closure rate, report approval cycle time, scope-quarantine rate, reuse of engagement templates, and analyst time spent locating relevant context. It should not optimize for number of targets, scan volume, or activity against systems.

## Competitive positioning

Aegis Atlas should be positioned as a **governed assessment-intelligence workspace**, not a scanner, vulnerability feed, military-style tracking product, or surveillance system. The core business promise is faster, more defensible assessment decisions with better geographic and evidence context.
